## The defects

**D2 (calendar, most important): the open popover inherited the out-of-month cell's 0.45 opacity.**
- Before (09-23, `/calendar?month=2026-09`): the Oct 1 "Monthly update" bubble had the cell as a dimmed
  ancestor, ran 253px past the grid and overlapped the legend. The Aug 31 ESPP bubble was also *under*
  the next rows' chips (hit test 22–23/25 points, because the dimmed cell is its own stacking context).
  The in-month Sep 30 bubble (last row) overlapped the legend too.
- Fix (`CalendarPage.css`, `CalendarGrid.tsx`):
  - An out-of-month day recedes by its own parts: the date button, chips and "+N more" at
    `opacity: 0.45`, and its border mixed to 45% (`color-mix`, with a plain-line fallback first, the
    house convention). The cell itself is no longer dimmed.
  - An out-of-month *today* keeps its dimmed accent border (`.cal-day-outside.cal-day-today`).
  - Bubbles in the grid's lower half (`weekIndex >= weeks.length / 2`) open upward (`.cal-popover-up`),
    mirroring the existing right-two-columns rule.
- After: all 16 runs (4 bubbles × 2 themes × 1280/1600) have no dimmed ancestor, a 25/25 hit test
  (on top), the bubble inside the grid, and no legend overlap. Out-of-month chips still measure 0.45.
  With an unchanged bubble open (Sep 15), before vs after is pixel-identical (max channel delta 2/255)
  in both themes at both widths, so the dimmed cells look exactly as they did.
- Tests: `calendarCss.test.ts` checks that no dimming rule's subject is the cell, the chip slot or the
  popover, and pins the up rule. `CalendarGrid.test.tsx` checks the lower half opens upward (a five-row
  September and a six-row August), rows above open downward, and right-anchoring still composes.

**D3 (Overview › Up next): the date wrapped ("Oct 1," over "2026").**
- Fix: `.up-next-date { flex: none; white-space: nowrap }`, so the label wraps instead.
- Before: "Oct 1, 2026" took 2 lines at 1280 and 1600 ("Oct 15, 2026" too at 1280). After: every date
  is on one line at 1280/1600/1920, and row heights are unchanged.
- Pixel diff: the only changes are in the reflowed rows. 1920 is identical.
- Test: `overviewCss.test.ts`.

**D4 (Overview › Data status, 10-16): "Balances as" over "of".**
- Fix: `.data-status-row dt { flex: none; white-space: nowrap }`, so the value wraps.
- After: every label is on one line at 1280/1600/1920. At 1600 only that row changes; 1920 is identical.
- At 1280 the label's regained width costs the value a third line, so the card grows 18px. The stretched
  Changes card matches it (shared bottom) and the cards below shift 18px
  (`shots/geometry/d4-{before,after}-1280.json`). This is inherent to "label one line, value wraps" in
  the 348px agenda column.
- Test: `overviewCss.test.ts`.

**D5 (Projection at 1280): tiles wrapped 4 + 1.**
- Probed first (`shots/d5-probe/`): five across does not fit a 991px page. "Investable balance ⓘ" runs
  15px past a 185px tile's content box and the values shrink to 15.5px, while 3 + 2 fits with room.
- Fix (`ProjectionPage.css`): `@container page (min-width: 660px) and (max-width: 999px)` puts the band
  on six tracks, each tile spanning two and the last two spanning three.
- After at 1280: 3 + 2 (tiles 320/320/320 over 487/488), band 236px (was 270), nothing overflows.
  1440/1600/1920 are pixel-identical (still five across).
- Edges: a 999px page gives 3 + 2 and 1000px gives five (shared rule). A 671px page gives 3 + 2; below
  660px the shared auto-fit takes over.
- The 660–847px band changed on purpose: the auto-fit already gave 3 + 2 there, but with an empty third
  slot on row 2. The same rule now fills that row, so narrowing past 848px no longer changes the shape.
- Test: `projectionCss.test.ts`.

**D6 (Monthly update › Review footer, 10-03): the gate sentence wrapped flush against "Save progress".**
- Before: 0px between the sentence's glyphs and the buttons, 2 lines, at 1280 and 1600. October's
  early-balances sentence did the same.
- Fix: the sentence takes its own line, right-aligned above the button row (`flex-wrap`, note
  `flex: 1 0 100%`). The actions keep the right edge (`margin-left: auto`). The sentence also moved
  first in the footer markup, so reading order matches visual order.
- After: one line, 8px above the row at both widths, actions flush right, Back left. The footer is
  24px taller.
- The Balances and Spending footers, which share `.wizard-footer`, measure identical before/after
  (`shots/d6-footers/`).
- Tests: `MonthlyUpdatePage.test.tsx` (footer order) and the new `monthlyUpdateCss.test.ts`.

## Decisions

- D2: "must not overlap the legend" was read literally, because the reminder always sits in the last
  row. The flip uses the lower half rather than the bottom two rows: five-row months get the same rows
  either way, and six-row months need row 4 up as well.
- D6: the sentence goes above the buttons, not beside them with a gap. Above, it fits one line at 1280
  and 1600; beside, it needs two lines at both widths (orphaning "appropriate." at 1600) and would get
  worse as the page narrows.
- D5: the skeleton's ghost row is the generic `.kpi-row`, outside the page's band, so it was left alone
  (the calendar strip's 880px rule set that precedent).
