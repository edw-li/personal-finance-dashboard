## Decisions this plan takes (recorded again in the execution record)

1. **`starts_on` (pace walk)** = the earliest profile's date when at least one payday inside the
   window was on or before it (so Edward's Jan 1 profile yields null; Grace's Sep 1 yields Sep 1).
2. **`first_payday_passed`** follows the first *credited* payday: a job that starts Sep 1 has its
   employer HSA deposit "ride" its first real check (Sep 15), not Jan 15. A direct consequence of
   W1's "nothing counts before"; no change for anyone whose first profile predates Jan 15.
3. **Calendar future-only profile:** `test_calendar_falls_back_to_a_future_only_profile` is
   rewritten — a profile that starts Dec 1 emits no August paydays any more (W1: nothing on or
   before the first profile's date); its December paydays still show.
4. **Early-check warnings** carry the person's name ("Grace's checks on or before Sep 1, …");
   without a roster name they read "Your checks …" / "Your partner's checks …".
5. **Grids on the wire:** `WithholdingOut.grids: list[WithholdingGridOut]` — one per simulated leg
   (primary; partner when simulated) with the W2 facts and the exact early-check sentence, so the
   card can show that sentence inline and keep it out of the method disclosure by string equality.
6. **Reconciliation pricing** is a callback from the router (it owns `_stored_slot`), so the
   service stays pure and the preview's adoption rule has one home.
7. **Primary with no profile:** its note reads "{name} has no paycheck profile, so their paycheck
   inputs are not reconciled — add one on the Paycheck page to project their salary" (the spec's
   sentence, "their withholding comes from the entered W-2 rows", is only true of a partner).
8. **RSU row without a ticker:** no row and a note ("RSU income is not reconciled: no employer
   ticker is configured") — a projection of $0 there is "unknown", not zero.
9. **Missing limits:** a note per missing cap ("No 2026 401(k) elective limit is stored, so the
   401(k) projection is not capped" / "… HSA {self-only|family} limit …").
10. **Additional-Medicare gap:** each person's own ESPP ordinary income leaves their Medicare wages
    (the spec names the primary; the partner's is the same rule and a no-op without partner ESPP).
11. **Sale view tiles:** with legs present the kpi row is exactly Proceeds · Tax due · Net cash ·
    After-tax gain; the totals and rates stay in the compare table below.
12. **Drafts** are cleared whenever the page's own discard confirm is accepted (year switch,
    status change, create, retry) and before an external write remounts the form (vest Apply,
    what-if Apply), so a draft never outlives a discard the user chose.
13. **Pace note once per panel:** the "Nothing counts before …" sentence renders once above the
    rows (one walk serves every row), not once per row as `backfilled_from` did.
